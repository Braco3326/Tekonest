> **Note, 24 Oct 2026.** This is a dated record. > Scope: colour only. Logo geometry, typography, page copy and layout are unchanged. The approved prototype (`website-prototype/`) and its `tokens.css` were **not** modified. Every option is rendered from a scratch copy with an override stylesheet (`tools/render.py`).
> Branch: `design/tekonest-umbrella-brand`. Nothing merged or deployed.

**Council question:** what colour system best expresses Tekonest as one brand across culturally mixed events, a creative/technical network, organisers, a learning platform and TekPractice, while staying minimal, modern, recognisable and accessible?

## How it was run (honest account)

- **expert-council** (Tier 3) was followed: independent round 1, a challenge round 2 with explicit `[POSITION CHANGE]` lines, a validator pass, then a neutral synthesis (below).
  - **Deviation:** the skill says to generate fresh personas. To respect your rule of no new agents without a name from you, the three expert seats were filled by your existing agents: **tekcreativedirector**, **tekbranddesigner** and **tekdesigner**. **tekreviwer** is the validator. The neutral synthesis is mine (orchestrator).
- **deep-research** was used for one question only: colour clichés and cultural/trade references. A researcher agent wrote the sourced notes in `research/`. The report-writer step was **not** run; the notes are used directly.
- **canvas-design / deep-scan:** I did not run them as separate skills in this pass. The boards use the canvas-design fonts. The gap-hunting job was done by the validator pass, which found 8 issues and led to the fixes listed below.
- **Checks on every option:** WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (text ≥ 4.5:1, non-text ≥ 3:1), CVD simulation, grayscale, and keyboard focus. Focus was checked on R only, by tabbing in Chromium.

## The baseline problems (what triggered the review)

1. **Sodium `#E8590C` has six jobs:** the logo plug, the coil strand, the focus ring, the active-nav underline, the Soirées cable, and the hue of the "en projet" status.
2. **Branch and status colours are identical in two places:** Réseau = "existe" (`#12775A`) and TekPractice = "en développement" (`#2E5FE6`).
3. **The Apprendre amber cable `#E8B04B` is 1.87:1** on paper, which fails the 3:1 non-text minimum.
4. **The branch hues collapse to L\* 41–46 in grayscale.** Violet and blue merge for protan/deutan viewers.
5. **Five hues cannot be reproduced** in one-colour or two-ink riso print.

### Strengths and weaknesses

| | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Cheapest. Fixes every compliance failure. Familiar | The renders look almost identical to today. Branch hues still fail one-colour print and cluster in grayscale (L\* 38–56). No night, no warmth |
| B | Most emotional range. The Soirées night hero proves warmth can come from a dark ground, not from hues | Sodium has thin margins on écru (3.26:1, and 3.10:1 on sunken). Five hues still fail print. The teal/raspberry/bronze set reads "heritage boutique". The second ink means asset churn |

## Council perspectives

**tekcreativedirector** (posterior confidence 78):
- Wants warmth from the night, not from hues.
- **Scores (sum):** A 22 · B 25 · C 28.
- **Position:** C frame + night for Soirées and default posters + one guest colour per night, chosen with the artists.
- **Blocks:** umbrella branch hues; pattern cables that reuse status shapes; a guest colour as default ground; cultural colour codes.

**tekbranddesigner** (82):
- **Scores (sum):** A 21 · B 24 · C 30.
- **Position change:** "C, B as fallback" → C frame + B night, single navy, paper unchanged, guest colour on posters only.
- **Trigger:** the B events render, the C poster breaking grid recognition, and the warmer paper failing the input border.

**tekdesigner** (78):
- **Scores (sum):** A 22 · B 23 · C 28.
- **Position change:** "A now, converge to C" → hybrid C-native directly.
- **Trigger:** C holds at 375 px, and native borders remove the implementation risk, so one migration beats two.
- Found the **focus-ring blocker:** ink on ink, 1:1 on the footer, in A, B and C.

**tekreviwer (validator):**
- **Passes:**
  - all 32 contrast claims recomputed within ±0.01;
  - colour is never the sole carrier of meaning in R;
  - the position changes cite evidence.
- **Flagged, and fixed in R since:**
  - The focus colour had been frozen at `:root`. It is now resolved per element; on the footer it renders `rgb(250,250,247)` on ink, 16.45:1.
  - The dotted "later" status collided with the dotted cable. "Later" is now a hollow square.
  - The double rule was under 6 px. It is now 6 px.
  - There were two oranges on the poster. It now uses one plug colour.
- **Flagged, documented here:**
  - Use `data-branch` instead of `[style*=]` when implementing.
  - The guest-colour rules (below).
  - Several cultural claims in the expert files (pan-African, "oriental" gold, the Paris sodium-lamp story) are **inferences, not sourced facts**. Paris has been moving from sodium to 2,700 K LED since 2011, so "sodium" is a memory, not today's street.
  - The round-2 confidences (78–82) are probably a little high while the pattern dispute is open.

### Consensus (all three experts + validator)
- Keep paper `#FAFAF7`, ink `#16163F` and sodium `#E8590C` as the brand core.
- Status = ink **shape + text label**, never hue.
- Focus ring ≠ sodium.
- No hue-coded branches on the umbrella (tekdesigner dropped his A position for this).
- Soirées gets the night ground.
- A guest colour may only appear per event/edition, never on the avatar or on recurring templates.
- Learning (dark amber) and TekPractice (light blue) keep their own product skins inside their previews.

### Dissent worth your attention
1. **Patterns vs plain rules** (tekcreativedirector): wants solid ink rules with no patterns at all, so labels alone identify branches. The others keep patterns as a second cue for print and mobile. R follows the majority.
2. **Night tone:** tekcreativedirector prefers aubergine `#1B1736` and a slightly warmer paper `#F8F6F1`. The validator measured the input border at 2.95:1 on that paper, a fail. R keeps one navy and the current paper.
3. **Default poster ground:** tekcreativedirector wants night by default, with the guest colour only "per night". tekbranddesigner allows a guest flood on that edition's poster. R defaults to navy; the guest colour is your decision (below).

### Recommendation
**R · Council hybrid.** Neutral synthesis, posterior confidence **72 %** (lowered from the experts' 78–82 because of the open pattern dissent).
- It is the only system that passes every check in the renders: contrast, CVD, grayscale, forced colours and keyboard focus.
- It survives one-colour print.
- It adds the night warmth that A lacks, without B's second ink or five hues.

## Exact token changes (R vs `website-prototype/assets/css/tokens.css`) — proposed, not applied

```css
/* unchanged: --color-paper #FAFAF7 · --color-ink #16163F · ink-2/3 · --color-signal #E8590C · --color-signal-text #B4430A */
--signal-on-dark: #FF8A4C;            /* NEW: sodium as text/accent on night — 7.37:1 on #16163F */
--focus-color: #16163F;               /* NEW: ink on paper; set to #FAFAF7 on .site-footer and night sections */
--focus-width: 3px;                   /* NEW: use `outline: var(--focus-width) solid var(--focus-color)` where focus is drawn — not a pre-composed :root --focus-ring */
--cable-soirees … --cable-tekpractice: #16163F;      /* all branch cables ink (were 5 hues) */
--cable-*-text: #16163F;
--cable-style: solid;                 /* NEW, per branch via [data-branch]: soirees solid · reseau double (≥6px) · organiser dashed · apprendre dotted · tekpractice dotted */
--status-now / -dev / -prep / -later: var(--color-ink); /* meaning by shape: ● existe · ◐ en développement · ○ en projet · □ plus tard */
```
Components:
- `.door`, `.signup`, `.branch-hero .cable` and `.board th a` use `border: 6px var(--cable-style) var(--color-ink)`.
- `.status::before` draws the four shapes, with a `forced-colors` fallback (`CanvasText`).
- The Soirées hero gets the night ground.
- The hard-coded `#26265C`, `#D2D2E4` and `#36366A` stay valid because the ink is unchanged.

**Guest-colour rules (posters/social only):**
- One guest colour per edition, chosen with the artists. Never a "cultural code".
- Never on the avatar, recurring templates or the website.
- Text on the guest colour ≥ 4.5:1. The plug on it must be ≥ 3.5:1, or else use an ink plug.
- Pale guests (G1–G4 from round 1) don't take an orange plug (1.5–1.7:1).
- G5 was dropped.

**Forbidden:**
- sodium on `#F2EEE7`, or on any ground where it is under 3.3:1;
- sodium as the focus ring or as a status hue;
- two oranges in one artefact;
- dotted/dashed status shapes;
- gradient-drawn cables;
- neon/acid green on black;
- lantern, mosaic, wax-print or kente fills used as "culture".
