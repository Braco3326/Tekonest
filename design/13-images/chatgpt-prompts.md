# Tekonest: image prompts for ChatGPT

Paste the **style block** at the start of a ChatGPT chat once. Then send the prompts one at a time.
Download each image at the largest size and send it back to Claude with its file name (e.g. `01-hero-nid.png`).

## Style block (send first)

> You are generating photos for TEKONEST, a Paris collective for music cultures (Arab, from the Middle East to Morocco; Afro; electronic) and the craft of sound.
> House style, apply to every image:
> - Cinematic documentary photo, 35mm, shallow depth of field, real textures, light film grain.
> - Palette: near-black night (#131114), warm amber light (#DD9349), deep plum (#612D45), paper cream (#F3EDE3). Mostly dark, one warm light source.
> - Lots of negative space (text will sit on top), subject off-centre.
> - No text, no letters, no logos, no calligraphy, no lanterns, no camels, no "oriental" clichés. No faces looking at the camera.
> - Damascus craft reference = inlaid wood + mother-of-pearl, geometric, used sparingly.
> Reply "ok" and wait for my prompts.
