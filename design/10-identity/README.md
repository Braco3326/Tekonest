# 10 — Identity direction (founder-approved, 24 Oct 2026)

Founder: "we are going to use these for design and identity and the colors — I love it." The board's "TECHONEST" spelling is an error: the name is **TEKONEST**.

`moodboard-founder.png` is the reference: the L4 nest logo, warm stage light, vinyl, crowds, a lanyard, merch, torn posters.

## Palette (sampled from the board)

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nuit | `#131114` | Dark ground, text on light |
| Papier | `#F3EDE3` | Light ground, text on dark |
| Crème | `#EAE2D5` | Secondary light surface |
| Ambre | `#DD9349` | Accent: logo on night, light, highlights |
| Prune | `#612D45` | Second accent: crowd and night imagery, bands, merch |

Contrast (WCAG): Papier/Nuit 16.1 · Ambre on Nuit 7.5 · Prune/Papier 9.2 · Ambre on Prune 4.2 (large text only) · **Ambre on Papier 2.2: never for text or the logo; use Prune or Nuit on light grounds.**

## Logo and type

- Logo L4 "nid tressé" (`../09-logo-nest/`), name in **uppercase TEKONEST**, Tektur.
- Variants: ink on paper, paper on night, amber nest on night.
- Imagery: warm, grainy, real nights (amber/plum duotone). AI board images are mood only, not usable photos.

Supersedes the colour options in `08-colour-review/` and the Bobine logo in `03-logos/`.
