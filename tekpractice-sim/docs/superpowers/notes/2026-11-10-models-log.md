
## Wave 0 - Outline port markers (2026-11-10)

Commits: 5e58e8c (marker size from the connector), a62bf57 (rings replace the 50 mm discs), 8d571ed (screen-space floor and highlight rule), 1093a09 (rings lifted out of connector pockets), plus this log.

### Before / after marker sizes
- Before: every marker was an opaque 50 mm cylinder (20 mm thick). Mode Creation shrank it only to 0.9 x the nearest catalog neighbour (floor 0.3, so 15 mm), a hovered or pending one grew to at least 50 mm. On the QL1 rear (XLR pitch 25.3 mm) three yellow discs overlapped three connectors and the pending etherCON disc was about 50 mm over a 24 mm connector.
- After: a flat double-sided outline ring (32 segments, 64 triangles, width 12 % of the diameter, at least 1.2 mm), outer diameter = connector size x 1.25 from `src/scene/portMarkerSize.ts`: XLR 30 mm, etherCON 30 mm, IEC C13/C14 37.5 mm, RJ45 20 mm, TRS 1/4" 17.5 mm, 3.5 mm jack 10 mm, BNC 17.5 mm, bantam 13.75 mm, DB25 / GPIO 50 mm, DigiLink 15 mm, unknown 25 mm. All 13 connector ids used by `content/catalog.json` are in the table (test lists any missing). restScale is 1 for every marker (metadata contract kept).
- Screen-space rule (`portProxies.ts`): a ring is raised to 9 CSS px outer diameter when far (`ringScale`, `RING_MIN_PX`), left at its own size otherwise; a hovered / pending / ok ring reads `HIGHLIGHT_PX` (14 px), never under its rest size, never above 1.6 x rest when the camera is closer than 1 m. At 6 m on the QL1: XLR ring x2.05 (61 mm), pending etherCON ring x3.2 (96 mm, 14 px).
- Click targets: the invisible pick spheres and their 12 px radius floor are untouched. `firstClickRadiusPx` and the tight radius in `portAt` now use the ring's real rest radius (15 mm for an XLR) instead of the fixed 25 mm of the old disc.

### Decisions
- The spec `openingMm` is not in the GLB extras (extras carry only portId and pos), so the runtime size comes from the connector table; `markerDiameterM(connector, openingMm?)` accepts it for later. The table has the same 24 mm for XLR as the specs.
- No 25 % fill in hover / pending states (spec 4.1 allowed it, did not require it): the colour change plus the 14 px growth reads clearly in the browser.
- Added beyond the plan: ports in a pocket (speaker amplifier plates: Genelec 13.8 mm, K12.2 25.8 / 27.5 / 38.9 mm, DBR12 about 25.6 mm, RE20 25.6 mm) sit on the pocket floor, so a flat ring there is hidden behind the panel (seen on the Genelec in the browser, no ring at all). On model load the spawner now probes the body (5 rays per port, once) and lifts the ring geometry by the recess it measures (`surfaceLiftM`, `liftMarkerRing`); the marker position (cable anchor) does not move. Measured lifts equal the spec insets (13.80, 27.49, 38.91, 25.80 mm). A flat panel, a curved body (mic tube), no surface, or a recess deeper than 6 cm measure 0 (unit tests).
- Marker materials: `backFaceCulling = false` and `zOffset = -2` (the ring lies on the connector's face).

### What was built
- New shape `curved-cabinet` (Blender `build_curved_cabinet`): the plan is a convex polygon (baffle corners, widest points, rear corners) whose corners are rounded by tangent arcs (front, side, back radius), stretched in x to the exact width; extruded with an elliptic fillet (edgeRadiusMm inward by edgeDropMm down, edgeSegments steps) on top and bottom; sides can be barrelled (an arc in elevation, intermediate wall rings, one exactly at the widest height); round recesses on the flat baffle (woofer ring, tweeter ring with an optional conical waveguide); round pockets in the rear face behind every inset port (so `portSurface` is real); an optional rubber foot; `bodyGrey` for the untextured faces. It measures the fillet it built (the smaller of the horizontal and the vertical radius, from the final vertices) into `<out>.measure.json`; `runBuilder` returns it, the fix-up writes it as `tekpractice.filletMm` in the GLB extras next to realSizeMm.
- Gates: `detail` (triangles inside `build.budget {min,max}`) and `fillet` (`filletMm` >= `build.minFilletMm`, fails closed on NaN and on a missing measurement). They are added to the gate list only for specs that declare them (the other 47 assets keep their 7 gates, so no manifest row or existing test changed). `npm run validate:assets` runs them too (`tools/validate-assets.ts`, the one file touched outside `tools/assets3d`).
- Params added beyond the plan's list (all in `SHAPE_SPECS`, mirrored in the Python builder, with TS checks): midWidthMm, rearWidthMm, midDepthMm, frontRadiusMm, sideRadiusMm, edgeDropMm, barrelMm, barrelCentreMm, footMm, footWidthMm, footDepthMm, bodyGrey, wooferYMm, tweeterYMm, ringDepthMm, waveguideMm, waveguideDepthMm. The plan's starting values did not survive the real photos: `backRadiusMm 60` leaves a rear flat of 69 mm, narrower than the two rear pockets need (103 mm), so 26 mm; `edgeRadiusMm 32` folds over any plan corner tighter than 32 mm and the woofer ring needs a nearly full-width flat baffle (front corner 20 mm), so the horizontal fillet is 18 mm with a 22 mm drop (the plan's "elliptic profile").
- Photo projection: new `,fill` suffix on `--project-photo` (`face=file@l,t,w,h,fill`): the near-white background connected to the border is repainted row by row with the nearest product colour (taken 4 px inside the edge, never an edge pixel that is a blend with white). Without it the white corners of a rounded product land as white specks on the fillets. Tested (corners filled, no change unless asked, a white product left alone).
- `BUILDER_VERSION` stays `blender-builder-1` (it is in the manifest strings of other assets and in a test).

### Genelec 8030C: what was compared with what
Photos: `front.jpg` (8030CP-k01), `rear.jpg`, `three-quarter-left.jpg`, `rear-three-quarter.jpg` (assets-source/genelec-8030c/photos). A row-by-row silhouette profile of front.jpg showed that the sides are NOT straight: 175 mm wide 39 mm below the top, 189 mm at about 140 mm below the top, 172 mm at 250 mm below it. That is why the shape has `barrelMm`. Woofer grille 137 mm across, centre 115 mm above the base; tweeter ring 41 mm at 233 mm (first put 15 mm too high because the pod-inclusive photo had been used for the scale; the render showed the ring above the picture's woofer and the measurements were redone on the cropped picture).
Iterations: (1) first geometry with the Meshy left picture: the left side showed a seam and white patches from the Meshy render and the photo's pod was painted on the bottom fillet: dropped the Meshy picture (sides are plain grey now), added the 14 mm Iso-Pod pad and cropped both photos to the body (front `501,195,985,1475`, rear `492,180,1068,1600`, both `,fill`); (2) woofer and tweeter heights and the logo blank rectangle (the old one `(70,34,50x22)` no longer matched the cropped picture: now `(61,20,58x16)`) corrected from the photo; (3) a ring exactly at the barrel centre so the width is exact in the raw builder.
Params ended on: `planFrontMm 180, sideBulgeMm 4.5, rearWidthMm 172, frontRadiusMm 20, backRadiusMm 26, edgeRadiusMm 18, edgeDropMm 22, edgeSegments 8, barrelMm 17.7, barrelCentreMm 159, footMm 14, footWidthMm 118, footDepthMm 100, bodyGrey 112, wooferRingMm 136, wooferYMm 115, tweeterRingMm 41, tweeterYMm 233, waveguideMm 66, waveguideDepthMm 8`; budget 1500 to 4000; minFilletMm 16 (measured 18.00).
Remaining differences, honestly: the real waveguide is a wide soft dish spanning most of the baffle and blending into the top, ours is a 66 mm cone (the rest is only the photo's shading); the real Iso-Pod is an X of two rubber bars, ours is one rounded pad; the top and the sides are plain grey (the real top blends into the front with a larger radius); a stepped boundary where the photo ends on the top fillet corner shows in close-ups; the plan taper to the rear is estimated from the 3/4 photos (no top view exists).
