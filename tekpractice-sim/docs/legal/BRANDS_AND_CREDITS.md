## 1. Brands and products shown in the simulator (permission page)

How each model was made:
- **Photo-textured**: our own scripted 3D shape, wearing the manufacturer's official product photos as its surface picture (front, rear, top; some straightened from a 3/4 photo). The brand's own artwork and printed legends are visible on the model. Logos and wordmarks were repainted out where noticed; the atlases were looked at again on 2026-11-11 (see the column "Brand marks on the model" and the manifest's `logosRemoved`, which is `false` on the three rows where a mark was seen to remain).
- **Meshy-generated**: generated by the Meshy AI service from the manufacturer's reference photos, then fitted to the real dimensions. Visible text and logos were repainted out where noticed. Today this covers the U 87 Ai only, plus the Meshy rear picture on the K12.2 (its shape is our own).
- **CC-BY download**: a third-party model under Creative Commons Attribution, modified by us (see section 2).
- **Generic**: our own scripted box at the real size with generic panel artwork (sockets, buttons, vents) drawn by us; no brand mark, not a copy of any photo.
- **Own work**: modelled by us from published dimensions.

## 4. AI generation

The Meshy-generated model (U87 Ai) and the Meshy pictures on the K12.2 rear were produced with a paid Meshy plan. Meshy states that paid plans own their generated assets provided the inputs do not infringe others' rights. The inputs were manufacturer photos: see section 1.

## 5. To ask for, per brand

1. Permission to show the product and its front, rear and top panel artwork in an educational simulator, with its name, free of charge, online.
2. Permission to use its official photos as surface pictures of our 3D model.
3. For Yamaha and Neutrik: written consent for use of CAD data as a measuring reference.
4. Whether the brand wants its logo shown (we currently repaint logos out) or a credit line instead.

## 6. Manifest reconciliation (every shipped device, from `public/assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.json`)

One row per device the simulator ships a model for (every manifest row of kind `device`), generated by `npm run register` (`tools/brandsRegister.ts`) from `public/assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.json` and `content/asset-specs/*.json`, so it cannot drift from them (a test fails when it does). Do not edit the table by hand. `tool` is how the GLB was made (scripted = our own Blender builder, hybrid = our own shape wearing a picture, meshy = generated by the Meshy AI service). `Dimensions source` is the page or document the real size was read from (the manifest `specSource`, else the first source of the device's spec). Devices built from the equipment plan (Waves 1 to 5) wear generic artwork drawn by our pipeline: no manufacturer photo, no logo, no Meshy. The connector models (XLR, TRS, RJ45, IEC, D-sub, etherCON and so on) are not listed: they are all `scripted`, `source: modeled`, `license: internal (own work)`, trademark `generic`, no picture.
