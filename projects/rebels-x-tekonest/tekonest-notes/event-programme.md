## 1. What it is and why it exists

- Culturally mixed nights in Paris, mostly Arab music and art "from the Middle East to Morocco", alongside Afro, electronic and other cultures **[F]** (EV-05).
- Founding belief: "diversity creates richness and harmony" **[F]** (EV-04).
- The founder describes it as the current event and cultural programming vision of Tekonest **[FB]** (FB-03).
- The nights should give artists and technicians fairly paid opportunities and real professional experience **[F]** (EV-07).

## 2. Who it is for, and what it answers

- **Audiences.** The existing docs treat these as hypotheses **[P]** (EV-12):
  - the house and Afro-house crowd around Rebels Collectif;
  - people who follow Arab/MENA live music;
  - students and creatives.
- **Performers and crew.** Artists and technicians who want paid work and credits **[F]** (EV-02, EV-07).
- **Unproven claims.** "Paris rarely mixes these scenes" and "young technicians struggle to get paid work" are unproven hypotheses. No demand data was found **[Inf]** (EV-09).

## 3. The experience

- **Proposed night format [P]** (EV-11, EV-32): live voice and percussion → a rehearsed crossover → a DJ programme. The crew films the night, and footage and credits go back to the creators.
- **Cultural principles [P]** (EV-10):
  - artists curate;
  - context, not costume;
  - real, rehearsed collaborations;
  - fair pay is part of the art.
- **Later.** Own event series, such as a themed year with Halloween and other themed nights **[F]** (founder Activity, see [event register §C](../evidence/event-project-register.md)).

## 4. Distinct identity and value

The nights would offer a curated cross-cultural line-up with live music at the centre, rather than a DJ-only club night. The intended edge is the combination of cultural curation, live collaboration and a visible, fairly paid crew **[P]** (EV-10, EV-11). Whether audiences value this enough to buy tickets has not been tested **[Inf]**.

## 6. What it would require

- **People.**
  - The founders.
  - Paid performers, presumed to be employees under French law **[R]** (EV-56).
  - Sound, light and film crew.
  - Security, with CNAPS-licensed agents; a specific card is needed above 300 people **[R]** (EV-75).
- **A legal organiser.** One entity holds the ticketing, the licence (récépissé) when required, insurance and payroll.
  - The rules were revised between the 20 Oct and 21 Oct docs.
  - The 21 Oct version: if live shows are the principal activity, a declaration is needed before the first show. GUSO is only for organisers whose main activity is not shows **[R]** (EV-59, EV-61).
- **Venue.** A club or rented hall with its own sound and security **[F]** (EV-34).
- **Equipment.** Rented, borrowed or the venue's own. School equipment only with written permission **[F]/[P]** (EV-78).
- **Tooling.** Ticketing and payroll **[P]**.

## 7. How it might sustain itself

- **Founder figures [F]** (EV-48, EV-36):
  - a target of 3 nights a month × 300 tickets × €15 = €13,500 a month;
  - €1,000 of his own money available.
- **Docs reading [E]** (EV-49): that figure is **gross ticket sales, not income**.
- **Pilot estimates [E]** (EV-41):
  - lean pilot (3 paid people) breaks even at ≈ 127 tickets;
  - base case (180 × €15) loses about €310.
- **Purpose of money [F]** (EV-51): pay study fees "when everything goes as planned", and recycle profits into projects.
- **Possible income lines [P]**: ticketing and a bar-share deal with the venue. Nothing is agreed.

## 8. Decisions, dependencies, risks, unknowns

- **Open decisions [I]** (EV-83):
  1. roles and time;
  2. decision and conflict rule;
  3. structure path;
  4. Rebels model and counterpart;
  5. pilot shape;
  6. money and fair-pay rules;
  7. artistic direction.
- **Conflicts to resolve.** The founder's earlier idea of unpaid or percentage pay for technicians conflicts with the docs' "everyone paid" rule and the legal basis behind it **[F] vs [P]/[R]** (EV-53, EV-54, EV-55). See [decisions register](../04-decisions-and-questions.md).
- **Dependency.** Any role the founder takes must fit the founder's personal work-status limits (details in [`event-project/docs/01`](../../event-project/docs/01-legal-structure-guide.md); not verified; not legal advice) **[R]** (EV-65, EV-66).
- **Main risks.**
  - **[Inf] judgment ratings** (EV-84):
    - too few tickets;
    - no legal organiser;
    - unpaid or percentage pay;
    - the founder's personal work-status limits;
    - injury;
    - Gabriel's double role.
  - Injury is also the founder's own biggest worry **[F]** (EV-77).
- **Stale material.** The 20 Oct docs (`02`, `03`) and the web deck carry older rules and numbers. The model's setup cost (€500) differs from the deck's (≈ €200) **[I]** (event register §F).
