## Change log

The deck was rebuilt, not polished: 23 slides became 26 main slides and 9 appendix slides, with speaker notes on every slide.

| Area | What changed | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrative | Built around two connected parts — the nights and the network — with an illustrative night, cultural principles, an audience and evidence ladder, and a creator pathway | The old deck explained rules better than it explained the idea |
| Licence | The récépissé valant licence is needed from the first show if live performance is the organiser's principal activity; the six-show allowance applies only when it is not | The old deck presented six shows as the whole rule |
| GUSO | Reserved for employers whose principal activity is not shows; covers live-show artists and technicians only — not filming, editing or marketing | The old deck said every paid role goes through GUSO |
| Association tax | Not automatic below €81,051: disinterested management, the 4P competition test and predominant non-profit activity also count; leaders' pay limits added | The old deck implied a simple threshold |
| Amateurs and volunteers | Amateur rules (convention, declaration, caps; technicians cannot be amateurs) added; fair pay kept as a team policy distinct from the legal rule | Requested distinction |
| Worker lending | Prohibition of for-profit lending (L8241-1) distinguished from a genuine production service, to confirm with a labour lawyer | Requested distinction |
| Financial model | Rebuilt with explicit formulas and four scenarios. Base case is now −€310, not +€240: pay estimated at €350 instead of €300, four paid people instead of three, gear €150 instead of €100 | More conservative inputs, all checked |
| Money concepts | Gross sales, net receipts, contribution, setup, cash before the event, maximum exposure and founder income now shown separately | They were blended before |
| Go / stop | Conflicting day −10 rules replaced by a D−14 continuation threshold (≥ 50 % of break-even) and a D−3 success target (≥ 75 %); cancellation costs made explicit (≈ €380 + contract fees) | The drafts disagreed |
| Pilot | A lean configuration (venue's own sound and security, 3 paid people) is now the recommended shape — the only one whose worst case fits the €1,000 cap when shared 50/50 | The base pilot's worst case is ≈ €2,330 |
| Wording | "An adviser recommends" removed; Rebels described only from public sources; Ahmad's performance marked as a suggestion | No identifiable adviser made recommendations |
| Design | Two diagrams, a timeline, a Gantt roadmap and two native charts; crowded "Three numbers" slide replaced by a 2 × 2 layout; compliance detail moved to the appendix | Readability when projected |
| Imagery | Abstract shapes only, no photographs | No supplied or properly licensed photos were available |
