14 · Protecting the 1 000 €

Four rules that cap the loss

No venue hire

Bar-share deal, with a cancellation clause in the contract

Stop at day −10

Under ≈ 90 presold tickets (60 % of break-even): cancel or downsize. Sunk cost then ≈ 300–500 €

Borrow, don’t buy

School or rented gear only; no equipment purchases in phase 1

Agree who covers a loss

In writing, before booking: Techonest alone, or shared with Rebels

Worst realistic case without these rules: ≈ 1 200 € on the night + up to 600 € of setup (estimate)

Without the rules, the cautious case loses about 1 800 € in total, almost twice what we have.
