16 · The pilot (proposal)

One test night to learn three things

The night

  • Live set, then Rebels DJs
  • Club or hall, 150–250 people
  • Between Jan and Mar 2027
  • Ticket around 15 €
  • 3–4 people paid through GUSO
  • Budget cap: 1 000 €

Success looks like

  • Break-even presales by day −10
  • Result ≥ 0 € after everyone is paid
  • No safety incident
  • A venue offers a second date
  • All three of us want to continue

We stop if

  • No legal organiser by Gate B
  • Costs above the cap
  • Presales under 60 % of break-even at day −10
  • A co-founder can’t give the time

We learn: do people pay for this mix · what it really costs · do we and Rebels work well together

Ahmad performing is a suggestion, not something he has agreed to. Date, price and size are decision fields.
