04 · Phase 1 scope

One job first: a few great, legal nights

In phase 1

  • One test night, then up to 6 in the season
  • Live music programmed next to DJs
  • Coordinating the crew for our own nights
  • Building a community list of artists and technicians

Not yet

  • Three events a month
  • Supplying technicians to other organisers for a margin
  • Buying equipment
  • Investment or loans

Lending workers to another business for profit is illegal outside authorised cases — Code du travail L8241-1

The technician service is the long-term idea, but it needs a legal set-up of its own (no profit on lending workers). Phase 1 is our own nights.
