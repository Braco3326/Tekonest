## 5. Rule ideas grounded in the sources (for the rule designer, not implemented)

- **R4 variant:** an active DI (`radial-j48`) or phantom-powered measurement mic outputs nothing without +48 V, same as a condenser.
- **R2 variant:** the same XLR carries analog or AES3 (`sonifex-dhy-04s`, `l-acoustics-la4x` inputs). Mismatch gives noise, not silence.
- **Speaker pinout:** d&b V-SUB uses NL4 2±, while the tops use 1±. A sub on the wrong pins gives "no sound from sub" (sources in `loudspeakers.json`).
- **Patchbay + phantom:** Royer and AEA warn against phantom through TT/TRS patch bays with ribbons.
- **Splitter:** only one console may send phantom to a split (`radial-js2`).
