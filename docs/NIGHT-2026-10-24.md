## 2. What works, and what doesn't

### Works (verified live on tekonest.com at about 11:50)

All URLs answer 200, with no console errors and no failed requests (`docs/night-2026-10-24/live-check.json`).

- **Click a device → the camera flies to it, and it becomes the centre of rotation.**
  - Live A1: a click on the console gives focus, and the pivot = the console's body centre (exact match).
  - Drag to turn around it; the wheel zooms toward the cursor; Shift/Ctrl+drag or right-drag pans; Alt+H or "Vue d'ensemble" returns home; a double-click on the floor re-pivots there.
- **View cube** (top-left; Orbit view):
  - Face / Arrière / Gauche / Droite / Dessus / Dessous, the four ¾ corners, rotate ±90° and home;
  - Numpad keys;
  - every action also has a named button;
  - live A1: "Arrière" keeps the console as pivot.
- **Vue 1/2/3:** right-click, long-press or "Enregistrer ici" saves; a click or keys 1/2/3 recall.
- **Essais** (sandbox):
  - write a prediction and press G to freeze;
  - change something and the caption says "Résultat à refaire";
  - freeze again to make a child Essai;
  - Comparer (B) flips A ⇄ B **at each Essai's stored camera**, read-only; Esc restores your setup exactly;
  - export and import `.essai.json`;
  - if stored data is ever unreadable, it is put aside and can be exported, never deleted.
- **Teacher page:** offline, reads the export, shows each Essai's prediction, changes, rules fixed/broken, notes and a 2D map, plus the "déclaratif" disclaimer and a **draft** rubric.
- **Data safety:**
  - rigs (v2), Essais and progress are never silently reset;
  - exam scores are now saved;
  - saving a rig under an existing name asks first.
- **Site:** the TekPractice page has "Jouer maintenant", a "Nouveau (24 octobre 2026)" section and French controls tables. The home "Se former" door links to the game.

### Doesn't work yet, or is limited

- **Phone (390 × 844):** the game loads and the cube shows, but in the live check a tap on the console **didn't focus it**: the aim point sat under the objectives card. Phones aren't a target (desktop ≥ 1366 × 768), but it needs a look.
- **Level A1 FPS:** a headless sample right after load gave 6.2 fps (models still loading); the sandbox gave 59 fps. **No real-GPU measurement yet.** Please try it on a school laptop.
- **Labels up close:** in focus, the device-name labels look very large. This is existing label scaling, made visible by the tighter framing (N9).
- **Not built tonight:** autosave of the live setup; editing note amendments in the UI; "exporter puis acquitter" (N19); phase 2 acoustics.
- **Final-review fixes:** done and redeployed (§6).

## 6. Final-review fixes (after deploy)

After the first deploy, tekreviwer's **whole-branch review** found no critical issues and three Important ones. All are fixed, re-reviewed and **redeployed at 12:39** (build `e8e489b`, site commit `52d1fba`).

1. **Ghost devices:** rapid compare flips could leave stray 3D models. Fixed in `DeviceSpawner`, with a reproducing e2e.
2. **Two tabs:** Essais saved in two tabs could overwrite each other. Saves now re-read and merge by id; a true conflict keeps this tab's copy and quarantines the other (exportable), with a visible warning. A trial deleted in another tab comes back after this tab's next write; this is documented in ADR-0012.
