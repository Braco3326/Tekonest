# Tekonest — umbrella brand vision

> **Working draft, 21 Oct 2026.** The founder is still developing the vision. This document separates what the founder has decided from what the existing documents suggest. Claude's recommendations are labelled **[Rec]**, and inferences are labelled **[Inf]**. Evidence IDs are listed in [05-sources-and-evidence.md](05-sources-and-evidence.md).

---

## 1. What the founder has decided

- **Name.** The brand is called **Tekonest** **[FB]** (FB-01).
  - Older spellings stay in historical files: Techonest, Technest, TechNest, Tech Nest, Teknest, TEKNEST, Thechnest.
- **Umbrella.** Tekonest is a brand or umbrella covering several services and ideas that "may connect over time". It is "kind of a nest of multiservices" **[FB]** (FB-02).
  - It is not one event, one website or one simulator.
- **Current visions [FB]** (FB-03, FB-04):
  - the [event & cultural programme](services/event-programme.md);
  - the [BTS Son learning platform](services/learning-platform.md);
  - [TekPractice](services/tekpractice.md).
  - The learning platform and TekPractice are under development during the website-creation phase.
- **One repository.** Everything about Tekonest lives in one repo, and each service has its own folder **[FB]** (FB-09).

## 2. How the brand grew (dated evidence)

| When | What "the brand" meant | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7–19 Jul 2026 | **"Tech Nest" / "Teknest"**: an AI-integrated platform to help students pass the BTS Audiovisuel option Son. Two products: a theory site (later "Tekskol") and a 3D simulator (later "TekPractice") | LT-01–LT-05 |
| 20–21 Oct 2026 | **"Techonest" → "Tekonest"**: culturally mixed Paris nights, plus a network giving artists and technicians fairly paid work | EV-01–EV-07 |
| 21 Oct 2026 | **Tekonest as an umbrella** holding both families and later services | FB-02–FB-04 |

The July documents never mention events, Rebels, a network or equipment. The October event documents never mention the learning products. The two strands were joined by the founder's 21 Oct instruction, not by an earlier plan **[I]** (thechnest register §D; event register §D).

## 3. Shared purpose (draft)

No source states a shared purpose for the whole umbrella. The candidate below is an **[Inf]** built from the founder's statements in both strands. It needs the founder's confirmation.

> *Tekonest helps people who love sound, image and live culture turn passion into real, fairly paid skill — by learning the craft properly, practising it for real, and working on nights that bring cultures together.*

## 6. Brand architecture: options, not yet decided

- **Current evidence [I].** Sub-names already exist: TekPractice, Tekskol, Tech Nest. Their shared "Tek" prefix suggests a family.
- **Founder decision needed.** The founder has not chosen how the services carry the name.

| Option | What it means | Fits if… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Branded house** | Everything is "Tekonest + descriptor" (e.g. Tekonest Nights, Tekonest Learn, Tekonest Practice) | The founder wants one strong name and one audience journey |
| **B. Endorsed family** | Own sub-brands "by Tekonest" (TekPractice, Tekskol, a name for the nights) | Audiences differ (students vs night-goers) but share one maker |
| **C. House of brands** | Separate brands; Tekonest is the company or workspace name only | The families should never be confused (e.g. a club night vs an exam-prep tool for possible minors) |

**[Rec]** Option B fits the current evidence best: two different audiences, with sub-brands that already exist. This is a recommendation only.

## 7. Boundaries (what Tekonest is not, today)

- **Not a legal entity yet.** No structure has been chosen **[I]** (EV-83).
- **Not in partnership with Rebels Collectif.** Nothing is agreed **[I]** (EV-27).
- **Not live with paying customers.** No service has any.
- **Not a staffing agency.** Supplying people for a margin is legally risky **[R]** (EV-76).
