# Tekonest — umbrella brand vision

> **Working draft, 21 Oct 2026.** The founder is still developing the vision. This document separates what the founder has decided from what the existing documents suggest. Claude's recommendations are labelled **[Rec]**, and inferences are labelled **[Inf]**. Evidence IDs are listed in [05-sources-and-evidence.md](05-sources-and-evidence.md).

---

## 1. What the founder has decided

- **Name.** The brand is called **Tekonest** **[FB]** (FB-01).
  - Older spellings stay in historical files: Techonest, Technest, TechNest, Tech Nest, Teknest, TEKNEST, Thechnest.
- **Umbrella.** Tekonest is a brand or umbrella covering several services and ideas that "may connect over time". It is "kind of a nest of multiservices" **[FB]** (FB-02).
  - It is not one event, one website or one simulator.
- **Current visions [FB]** (FB-03, FB-04):
  - the [artist & technician network](services/creative-network.md) and [production & crew services](services/production-and-crew-services.md) (*Créer avec nous* and *Rejoindre le réseau*);
  - the [BTS Son learning platform](services/learning-platform.md);
  - [TekPractice](services/tekpractice.md).
  - The learning platform and TekPractice are under development during the website-creation phase.
- **One repository.** Everything about Tekonest lives in one repo, and each service has its own folder **[FB]** (FB-09).

The two strands appear to have been joined by the founder's 21 Oct instruction rather than by an earlier plan **[Inf]**.

## 3. Shared purpose (draft)

No source states a shared purpose for the whole umbrella. The candidate below is an **[Inf]** built from the founder's statements in both strands. It needs the founder's confirmation.

> *Tekonest helps people who love sound, image and live production turn passion into real, fairly paid skill — by learning the craft properly, practising it for real, and working on real projects with a fairly paid team.*

## 6. Brand architecture: options, not yet decided

- **Current evidence.** Sub-names already exist: TekPractice, Tekskol, Tech Nest **[I]**. Their shared "Tek" prefix suggests a family **[Inf]**.
- **Founder decision needed.** The founder has not chosen how the services carry the name.

| Option | What it means | Fits if… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Branded house** | Everything is "Tekonest + descriptor" (e.g. Tekonest Learn, Tekonest Practice, Tekonest Network) | The founder wants one strong name and one audience journey |
| **B. Endorsed family** | Own sub-brands "by Tekonest" (TekPractice, Tekskol, a name for the network) | Audiences differ (students vs night-goers) but share one maker |
| **C. House of brands** | Separate brands; Tekonest is the company or workspace name only | The families should never be confused (e.g. a paid-crew network vs an exam-prep tool for possible minors) |

**[Rec]** Option B fits the current evidence best: two different audiences, with sub-brands that already exist. This is a recommendation only.

## 7. Boundaries (what Tekonest is not, today)

- **Not a legal entity yet.** No structure has been chosen **[I]** (EV-83).
- **Not in partnership with anyone yet.** [Rebels × Tekonest](../projects/rebels-x-tekonest/README.md) is a separate, idea-stage collaboration with Rebels Collectif; no agreement is signed **[I]** (EV-27). Its cultural-mix concept is not Tekonest's identity (D-08).
- **Not live with paying customers.** No service has any.
- **Not set up to supply workers.** The founder listed coordinating technicians for other organisers as an activity **[F]**, but the docs advise against supplying people for a margin, because of the French ban on for-profit worker lending **[R]/[Rec]** (EV-76). Not legal advice.
