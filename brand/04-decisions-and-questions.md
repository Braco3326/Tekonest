## 4. Top questions for the founder (max 7, in priority order)

1. **Brand architecture.** Should services carry "Tekonest" in their names, be sub-brands "by Tekonest" (TekPractice, Tekskol, a name for the network), or stay separate brands?
2. **The website.** In the "website-creation phase", which site do you mean: an umbrella Tekonest site, the learning platform, TekPractice, or one site holding several? Which domain?
3. **Ownership.** July files say the learning products "started as" another person's vision, and that "the project owner (diplômé BTS Son)" validated the content. Who owns these ideas and this code, and who validated it?
4. **Connection between the families.** Should BTS students and graduates connect to the network and the paid crews, or should the two families stay independent?
5. **Priority for the next 3 months.** Network and partner-project preparation (including Rebels × Tekonest), stabilising TekPractice and the learning site, or all of them? With what weekly time?
6. **Fair pay as a Tekonest-wide rule.** Do you confirm "everyone who works on a paid project is paid; no unpaid or percentage-based work" for all services?
7. **Learning-product pricing.** Free core (Dante-style), subscription, or school/B2B deals?
