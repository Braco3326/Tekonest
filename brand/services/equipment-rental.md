## 1. What it is and why it exists

- **Founder's idea [F].** Renting sound, light or video equipment to other organisers was listed as one of his activities. For now, equipment is rented or borrowed, including some from his school, with a plan to invest in owned gear later.
- **Why [Inf].** Owned gear could cut the cost of Tekonest's own nights and earn rental income.

## 2. Who it is for

Other organisers and the event programme itself **[F]/[Inf]**.

## 3. What exists today vs what is proposed

| Item | State |
| --- | --- |
| Owned equipment | None |
| Equipment used now | Rented or borrowed; school gear only with written permission **[F]/[P]** (EV-78) |
| Rental offer | Not started; "phase 3+", "equipment only when it beats renting", no purchases in phase 1 **[P]** |

## 4. What it would require

- **Purchase capital** **[Inf]**.
- **Insurance** for owned and rented-out gear **[P]**.
- **Running costs [Inf]:**
  - storage and transport;
  - maintenance;
  - rental contracts and deposits.
- **A legal entity** able to invoice. The founder's personal work-status limits (details in [`event-project/docs/01`](../../event-project/docs/01-legal-structure-guide.md); not verified; not legal advice) apply **[R]** (EV-66).

## 5. How it might sustain itself

- **Income [Inf]:** rental fees.
- **Savings for own nights [Inf]:** would only count once gear is used often enough to "beat renting" **[P]**.
- **Figures:** none discussed.

## 6. Decisions, dependencies, risks, unknowns

- **Depends on:**
  - a legal structure;
  - capital;
  - enough event volume.
- **Risks [Inf]:**
  - damage;
  - idle stock;
  - insurance cost.
- **Unknown:**
  - what gear;
  - for whom;
  - at what price.

## 7. Claude's recommendation (not a founder decision)

- **[Rec]** Record each equipment rental cost at the pilot and the nights after it. That record shows when owning would beat renting.
