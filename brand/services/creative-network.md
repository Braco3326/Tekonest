## 1. What it is and why it exists

- The founder's original Tekonest idea was to "connect technicians and give opportunities to technicians who don't find work and love événementiel" **[F]** (EV-02).
- The fields covered are filming, editing, marketing, sound, image, DJs and musicians **[F]** (EV-03).
- On 21 Oct the founder described this network as one of two connected parts of the project, alongside the events. It is a trusted network that helps artists and technicians find, prepare for and deliver paid creative and technical projects **[F]** (EV-06).

## 2. Who it is for, and what it answers

- **Who.** Early-career and student technicians, DJs, musicians, filmers, editors and marketers **[F]** (EV-02, EV-03).
- **What it answers.** The difficulty of getting paid, credited work and real experience **[F]** (EV-02, EV-07). That this need is widespread is an untested hypothesis **[Inf]** (EV-09).

## 3. The experience

- **Creator pathway, 8 steps [P]** (EV-14): discovery → selection → briefing → contract and pay → the night → credits and footage → feedback → next opportunity.
- **Build-up.** An "artist and technician call" and a "network list v1" (Apr–Jun 2027), built in parallel with the pilot **[P]** (event register §C).
- **Two earlier, separate "community" ideas** were attached to the learning products in July 2026:
  - BTS graduates become "community + mentor content" (TP-11, LT-24 **[P]**);
  - a "chercheur de stages/alternances" (internship/apprenticeship finder) is announced in the learning platform's E6 stub (LP-35 **[P]**).
  - Neither has been specified as part of this network. Whether they belong here is an open question **[Inf]**.

## 4. Distinct identity and value

- A small, trusted, fairly paid circle where people grow through real paid projects, with credits and footage they can show **[F]/[P]** (EV-06, EV-07, EV-14).
- What sets it apart from a generic job board is curation, briefing, and a commitment to pay **[Inf]**.

## 6. What it would require

- **People.** Someone who curates, briefs and follows up with members **[Inf]**.
- **Records.** A members list with consent for storing personal data. GDPR applies **[Inf]**.
- **Paid work.** At first that means Tekonest's own events **[P]** (EV-06, EV-14).
- **Payment rules.** When the network supplies people to other organisers, French law bans for-profit lending of workers (Code du travail L8241-1). A genuine production service or direct hire by the organiser may be lawful. This needs a labour lawyer **[R]** (EV-76). Not legal advice.

## 7. How it might sustain itself

Not discussed beyond the events themselves. The docs place coordination for other organisers "later, assessed separately" **[P]** (event register §C). No membership fee or commission has been proposed. A commission on supplying workers could fall under the L8241-1 risk above **[R]/[Inf]**.

## 8. Decisions, dependencies, risks, unknowns

- **Decision.** Is the network only Tekonest's own crew pool, or an open community? **[Inf]**
- **Decision.** Does it include the BTS student/graduate community from the learning products? **[Inf]**
- **Dependency.** It needs paid work to offer, which for now means the event programme **[Inf]**.
- **Risk.** The founder's earlier unpaid or percentage pay idea **[F]** (EV-53) conflicts with "fairly paid" **[F]** (EV-07) and with employment law **[R]** (EV-55, EV-56).
- **Unknown.** Size, entry criteria, who runs it, tools.

## 9. Claude's recommendations (not founder decisions)

- **[Rec]** Start with a simple, consented list of people who have actually worked a Tekonest night. Grow it only through paid work.
- **[Rec]** Keep "placing people with other organisers" out of scope until a labour lawyer has confirmed a lawful model.
