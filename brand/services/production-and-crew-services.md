**Why one document covers two ideas.** The founder listed "finding and coordinating technicians for other organisers" as an activity **[F]**, and named filming, editing and marketing among the network's fields **[F]**; his 21 Oct brief also mentions "a production service for other organisers" later **[F]** (event register §C). The docs found that supplying people for a margin risks illegal worker lending. A genuine production service, with its own know-how, supervision and price, is the form that "may be lawful" **[R]** (EV-76). Both activities are therefore treated here as one possible service, whose legal form is still open.

---

## 1. What it is and why it exists

- **Crew coordination.** Provide or coordinate sound, light, film, editing and marketing crews for events run by *other* organisers **[F]** (event register §C).
- **Partner projects.** Technical coordination for a partner project such as [Rebels × Tekonest](../../projects/rebels-x-tekonest/README.md), paid as a fee, is listed as a possible early, low-cost test **[P]** (register §C, plan §H). No agreement exists.
- **Why.** To create more paid work for the network beyond Tekonest's own nights **[Inf]** (from EV-02, EV-06).

## 2. Who it is for

- Organisers, venues and collectives that need a crew **[F]/[P]**.
- Network members who need paid work **[Inf]**.

## 3. The experience (as proposed)

- **The client books a service, not people.** The client pays a defined price for an outcome, such as a filmed and edited night or a coordinated technical setup. Tekonest supervises the work **[P]/[R]** (EV-76).
- **Alternative: "introductions".** The organiser hires and pays the technicians directly **[P]** (event register §C).

## 5. What it would require

- **A legal entity able to invoice.** The docs say an SAS is the usual tool for "a production service". The founder's personal work-status limits restrict what he can lead or earn **[R]** (EV-66, EV-67).
- **A labour-law check** of the L8241-1 boundary before any offer **[R]** (EV-76). Not legal advice.
- **Insurance, contracts, and equipment** (see [equipment rental](equipment-rental.md)) **[Inf]**.

## 6. How it might sustain itself

A price per production or a coordination fee **[P]**. No figures have been discussed.

## 7. Decisions, dependencies, risks, unknowns

- **Depends on:**
  - a legal structure;
  - a lawful model;
  - a reliable crew from the [network](creative-network.md).
- **Risks:**
  - illegal worker lending if the service is really just supplying people;
  - employment requalification if pay is informal **[R]** (EV-55, EV-56, EV-76).
- **Open question:** Rebels' interest and any possible conflict of interest through Gabriel **[F]** (EV-20).

## 8. Claude's recommendation (not a founder decision)

- **[Rec]** Keep this proposed until a first project has shown that the crew loop works and a lawyer has approved the model.
