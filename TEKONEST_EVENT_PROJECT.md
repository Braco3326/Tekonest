# Tekonest Event Project

> Where cultures meet on a Paris stage — and the people who make the night get real, fairly paid work.

**Status (21 Oct 2026):** idea stage · no legal structure yet · no agreement with Rebels Collectif · all figures are estimates until replaced by quotes · nothing here is legal advice.

---

## 1. The idea

Tekonest has two connected parts:

1. **The nights** — culturally mixed events in Paris with a clear artistic point of view: Arab music and art from the Middle East to Morocco, alongside Afro, electronic and other cultures.
2. **The network** — a community of artists and technicians (sound, lighting, image, filming, editing, marketing, DJs, musicians) who find, prepare for and deliver paid creative and technical projects.

Each night creates paid slots, credits and footage for the network; the network brings skills, new acts and audiences back to the nights.

**Belief:** diversity creates richness and harmony.

## 4. The pilot night (proposal)

| Item | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Format | Live voice and percussion → rehearsed crossover → DJ programme |
| Size | 120–200 people |
| Venue | Club or rented hall with its own sound and security, bar-share deal, cancellation terms |
| Staffing | Lean: 3 paid people + the venue's own staff |
| Timing | February–March 2027 |
| Ticket | €12–18, paid presale in two tiers |
| Budget cap | €1,000 maximum at risk |
| Continue rule | D−14: paid presales ≥ 50 % of break-even, else cancel or downsize |
| Success target | D−3: paid presales ≥ 75 % of break-even; night ends ≥ €0 after everyone is paid |

**Key numbers (estimates):** lean pilot breaks even at ≈ 127 paid tickets at €15; base pilot at ≈ 211. The long-term aspiration — 3 nights a month × 300 tickets × €15 = €13,500 — is gross ticket sales, not income.

## 5. Ground rules

- On paid nights, everyone is paid — no unpaid or percentage-based work.
- Paid performers are employees of the organiser; filming, editing and marketing roles need their own treatment.
- More than 6 shows a year, or live shows as the main activity, requires the licence (récépissé valant licence).
- Oscar's paid work stays within 964 hours a year on his student titre de séjour; no micro-entreprise.
- School equipment only with written permission from the school.
